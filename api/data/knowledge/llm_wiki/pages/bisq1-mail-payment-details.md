## Cash by Mail and postal money orders

For Cash by Mail, the seller needs deliverable recipient details and the buyer needs a usable return address; a permitted name or nym does not make an undeliverable address acceptable. USPMO app labels and the physical money order are different: follow USPS completion and cashing requirements, and do not copy the seller’s name into both sender and recipient fields as a shortcut. Follow the method’s current evidence and tracking rules as well as the agreed offer terms; photos or video do not replace correct delivery details. Use a correctly specified account for future offers when details change, and resolve active-contract changes with mediation. These methods do not gain account-signing status merely by completing a buy; do not promise Bisq 2 signed-age reputation from such a trade.
